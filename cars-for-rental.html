Good to know

Rental questions, answered

What do I need to hire a car?
A full, valid UK driving licence held for at least 12 months, a second form of ID (such as a recent utility bill or bank statement), and a card for the refundable deposit. Drivers must be aged 23 or over. Additional drivers can be added — just bring their licence too.
Is insurance really included?
Yes. Every hire comes with comprehensive insurance and nationwide breakdown cover as standard, for the full length of your rental. A standard excess applies; you're welcome to ask about reducing it when you book.
Where do I collect and return the car?
From our base right here in Chesham. We'll walk you round the vehicle together at handover and again on return, so everyone's clear. Out-of-hours collection can usually be arranged — just let us know your plans.
Are there mileage limits?
No caps for local driving across the Chilterns and the home counties. If you're planning a longer road trip — Scotland, the coast, or further afield — just mention it when booking and we'll set a fair allowance.
What about fuel and deposit?
Cars are supplied with a full tank; please return it full or we'll refuel and charge at cost. A refundable deposit is taken at collection and returned promptly once the car's back and checked over.
Can I add a child seat?
Of course. Child and booster seats are available on request at no fuss — just tell us the ages of your little ones when you book and we'll have them fitted ready for collection.
