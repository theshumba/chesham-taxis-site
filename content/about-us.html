Our story

Started in Chesham, here to stay

We began the way most good things round here do — with neighbours helping neighbours get where they needed to be. A reliable lift to the early train, a safe ride home after a night out in Amersham, a stress-free run to Heathrow before the holidays. Word spread, the fleet grew, and Chesham Taxis became the name local people pass on to a friend.

Today we run a modern, well-kept fleet across the Chilterns, but the promise hasn't changed. When you call us you reach people who live here too — who know that the Underground winds down at Chesham, that the school run on the A416 needs a head start, and that a quoted fare should be exactly what you pay at the kerb.

Why the red kite

The bird that came home to the Chilterns

Look up almost anywhere around Chesham and you'll see them — red kites wheeling on the thermals, russet wings catching the light. Once all but lost from Britain, they were brought back to these very hills and now soar over our towns and villages in their hundreds. They've become the unofficial emblem of the Chilterns.

We took the red kite as our own because it stands for everything we want to be: at home in this landscape, sure of its way, and a familiar, welcome sight overhead. Our forest green speaks to the beech woods and rolling hills; our red-kite orange is a nod to the bird that shares our skies.
