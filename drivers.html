The people behind the wheel

Every Chesham Taxis journey is in safe, local hands. Our drivers are DBS-checked, fully licensed by Buckinghamshire Council, and know the Chilterns like the back lanes of their own street. Friendly, smartly turned out and always on time.

Drivers you can trust

Vetted, licensed and properly local

When you climb into one of our cars, you're with someone who has been checked, trained and chosen for the way they treat people — not just for being free that evening.

Enhanced DBS-checked

Every driver passes an enhanced Disclosure and Barring Service check before their first shift — and we keep it current. Peace of mind for solo travellers, families and school runs alike.

Fully licensed

All our drivers hold a current private-hire licence issued by Buckinghamshire Council, with valid insurance and an MOT-tested, regularly inspected vehicle. Badges on display, always.

Real local knowledge

From the quickest cut through to Amersham-on-the-Hill to the right drop-off at Heathrow Terminal 5, our drivers know the roads. No staring at a sat-nav while the meter ticks.

What every Chesham Taxis driver signs up to

  • Smart, presentable appearance and a clean, valeted vehicle on every shift
  • Polite, patient service — help with bags, child seats and anyone less steady on their feet
  • Punctual pickups, with a courtesy call or text when we're outside
  • Honest, fixed-quote fares — never a longer route to bump the price
  • Calm, safe driving that suits the passenger, whether it's an early flight or a night out
  • Full respect for your privacy, your time and your destination
Meet the team

Familiar faces, year after year

A lot of our customers ask for the same driver — and we love that. Because we're a settled, independent firm rather than a faceless app, our drivers stick around, build a rapport and genuinely look out for regulars.

That continuity is why nervous flyers trust us with the 4am airport run, why parents are happy for us to do the school pickup, and why so many Chesham businesses keep a corporate account with us. You're a name to us, not a fare number.

Drive with Chesham Taxis

Join a friendly local team

We're always glad to hear from good drivers in and around Chesham. Steady local work, a fair deal and a team that actually picks up the phone when you need them.

Why drivers join us

  • Flexible hours — days, evenings, weekends or the airport early shift; you tell us what suits
  • Steady local work — a busy, established book of regulars, accounts and station runs across the Chilterns
  • A friendly team — a real office, real people, and dispatch that has your back
  • Fair, transparent pay — clear terms, prompt settlement, no surprises
  • Drive your own car or one of ours — whatever works best for you

What you'll need

  • A full UK driving licence held for at least 12 months
  • A Buckinghamshire Council private-hire driver licence (or willingness to apply — we'll guide you)
  • An enhanced DBS check (we can help arrange this)
  • Your own licensed, insured vehicle — or take one of ours
  • Good local knowledge and a warm, professional manner
