Pricing questions

Fares, payment & quotes — answered

How do I get a price before I travel?
Just call us on 01494 000000, email bookings@cheshamtaxis.co.uk or use our online booking form with your pick-up, destination and rough time. We'll come straight back with a clear, fixed quote so you know the cost before you set off.
Do you charge more at night, weekends or on bank holidays?
We don't use surge pricing. Our rates stay fair and consistent across busy nights, weekends and holidays. There may be a modest tariff for very late-night and public-holiday travel, but it's always reasonable, always agreed up front, and never a surprise multiplier.
Why might my quote differ from the sample fares?
The examples on this page are guide prices for a standard saloon. Your own quote can vary with your exact pick-up and drop-off, the time of day, how many passengers and bags you have, and whether you need a larger vehicle. We confirm your fixed price when you book.
Is there a charge for waiting or extra stops?
Every fare includes a short grace period of waiting time. For longer waits, multi-stop trips or wait-and-return journeys, our hourly hire rate is usually the best value — we'll talk you through the options when you book.
What happens to my airport fare if my flight is delayed?
We track your flight, so if it lands early or late we adjust your pick-up to match. You won't be charged extra simply because your plane was delayed — that's exactly the kind of stress we're here to take off your hands.
Can I set up a regular or account booking?
Absolutely. Whether it's a daily school run, a weekly station trip or a corporate account billed monthly, we'll set up a regular booking and a fare that works for you. Get in touch and we'll arrange it.

Want your exact price?

Tell us where you're heading and we'll send back a clear, fixed quote — no obligation, no surprises. We're here 24 hours a day, every day of the year.
